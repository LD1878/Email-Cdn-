DEFINING THE WIN

A 90-day sprint to show undeniable ROI for Halifax and Gloucester.

We want to set a benchmark so high that rolling this out to the remaining 78 locations is an easy decision. We measure success by ranking these goals:

  • Direct Sales: Increase in booked tickets and party inquiries directly from website visitors.
  • New Customer Discovery: Growth in traffic from parents searching for solutions (e.g., "kids indoor activities"), not just the brand name.
  • Local Map Dominance: Showing up first on Google Maps for local directions and weekend ideas.

*Baseline Requirement: We need baseline website traffic and booking data from the previous quarter before we launch.

THE WEBSITE STRUCTURE

OUR STRATEGIC ADVANTAGE

Your locations are built on separate subdomains (e.g., halifax.clipnclimb.co.uk) rather than folders. Google treats these as entirely separate websites. Halifax and Gloucester don't automatically inherit the massive search engine trust of the main brand.

Our Approach:

This is a massive opportunity for hyper-local focus. We treat Halifax and Gloucester as independent, local powerhouses. We will build highly specialized authority for these sites so they completely dominate their specific towns and shut out nearby competitors.
