DEFINING THE WIN
(HOW WE MEASURE SUCCESS)

A 90-day sprint to show undeniable ROI for Halifax and Gloucester.

We want to set a benchmark so high that rolling this out to the remaining 78 locations is an easy decision. We need to get them to rank the following in order of importance to their business goals:

  • A) Direct Sales: An increase in booked tickets and party inquiries coming directly from website visitors.
  • B) New Customer Discovery: Growth in traffic from parents searching for solutions (e.g., "kids indoor activities Gloucester") rather than just searching for your brand name.
  • C) Local Map Dominance: Showing up first on Google Maps when locals are looking for directions or weekend ideas.
Baseline Requirement: To prove our impact over the next 3 months, we will need your baseline website traffic and booking data from the previous quarter before we launch.

THE WEBSITE STRUCTURE

OUR STRATEGIC ADVANTAGE

During our initial technical review, we noted that their locations are built on separate subdomains (e.g., halifax.clipnclimb.co.uk) rather than folders on the main site.

Why this matters: Google treats these as entirely separate websites. This means Halifax and Gloucester don't automatically inherit the massive search engine trust of the main Clip 'n Climb brand.

Our Approach:

We view this as a massive opportunity for hyper-local focus. Instead of relying on the national brand, our strategy treats Halifax and Gloucester as independent, local powerhouses. We will build highly specialized authority for these sites so they completely dominate their specific towns and shut out nearby competitors.

INTERNAL 90-DAY ROADMAP
(TEAM MILESTONES)

We are compressing a standard 6-month SEO retainer into a 90-day aggressive sprint. To protect our agency margins, we will heavily utilize our AI-assisted workflows for data analysis and content frameworks. Here is our bi-weekly execution plan:

WEEKS 1-2

Technical Foundation & GTM Tracking
  • Tracking: Configure GTM/GA4. Set up custom event tracking specifically for "Book Now" clicks and form submissions. Establish the baseline metrics.
  • GBP Overhaul: Audit Google Business Profiles. Add UTM parameters to website links, seed localized FAQs, and correct category classifications.
  • Low-Hanging Fruit: Use Ahrefs/Semrush to identify "Striking Distance" keywords (positions 11-20). Optimize existing H1s/Meta tags to push them to Page 1.
Client Deliverable: Kickoff report establishing baseline numbers and detailing the immediate quick-wins implemented.

WEEKS 3-4

Local Content Deployment
  • Content Engine: Deploy high-volume local articles generated via our AI frameworks but human-edited for brand voice (e.g., "Top 10 Kids Birthday Ideas in Halifax").
  • Internal Linking: Ensure aggressive, optimized internal linking from these new informational assets directly to the commercial booking pages.
  • Technical Cleanup: Run Screaming Frog crawls. Fix 404s, redirect chains, and optimize Core Web Vitals (image compression, caching).
Client Deliverable: Action report detailing pages published, technical fixes, and initial indexation status.

WEEKS 5-6

CRO & Trust Building
  • Pain-Point Content: Publish deep-dive content targeting parent anxieties based on search volume (e.g., "Is indoor climbing safe for 5-year-olds?").
  • Local Citations: Run automated NAP (Name, Address, Phone) cleanup across primary UK directories to solidify entity trust for Google Maps.
  • CRO Review: Analyze Hotjar/Clarity heatmaps. If informational traffic isn't converting, we will propose UX tweaks to the "Book Now" flow and page layouts.
Client Deliverable: Progress report highlighting traffic shifts and specific CRO tweaks implemented.

WEEKS 7-8

Competitor Gap Execution
  • Gap Analysis: Run AI-assisted TF-IDF analysis against the top 3 ranking competitors in both regions. Identify missing semantic terms and inject them into our core pages.
  • Surrounding Suburbs: Publish a secondary wave of content specifically targeting geo-modifiers of surrounding towns and neighborhoods.
  • Link Acquisition: Begin outreach for local, high-relevance backlinks (local mom blogs, community event pages).
Client Deliverable: Market report showing competitor ranking displacement and our response strategy.

WEEKS 9-10

The Commercial Push
  • Sales Focus: Refine the commercial landing pages based on 60 days of real user intent data. Adjust CTAs and above-the-fold copy.
  • GBP Automation: Maximize Google Maps visibility by pushing weekly fresh updates, offers, and geo-tagged optimized images to their profiles.
  • Review Generation Strategy: Work with the client to ensure they have an aggressive in-store review generation process to support our map rankings.
Client Deliverable: Revenue report connecting the dots between organic traffic increases and direct booking completions.

WEEKS 11-12

The 80-Location Blueprint Assembly
  • Data Synthesis: Compile all traffic, ranking, and revenue data into a definitive case study for the Halifax and Gloucester locations.
  • Process Packaging: Document the exact workflows, content frameworks, and automation scripts that succeeded.
  • The Upsell Deck: Prepare the executive pitch for the remaining 78 locations, demonstrating economies of scale.
Client Deliverable: The Final Executive Report and the 80-Location Rollout Proposal.

OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

AI, AUTOMATION & MARGIN PROTECTION

Because we are running a heavily compressed timeline, manual execution will destroy our margins. We must leverage our AI stack as a strategic supercomputer:

  • Programmatic Research: We will use AI to scrape and analyze the local SERPs, extracting the exact entities, questions, and semantic structures the competitors are using.
  • Content Assembly Line: AI will generate the data-backed outlines. Our human writers will spend their time *only* on tone, brand voice, and engagement—drastically reducing cost-per-word.
  • Automated GBP Signals: We will use scheduling tools/APIs to ensure their Google Business Profiles are constantly fed new signals without requiring daily manual input from our team.

SPACING

CLIENT REPORTING ARCHITECTURE

Client retention relies on clarity. We will not send them raw SEMrush exports. We will use automated Looker Studio dashboards backed by brief, plain-English summaries every 14 days covering:

  • The Inputs: What we did (to show momentum).
  • The Outcomes: GA4 conversion data and GSC click data (to prove value).
  • The Next Move: Strategic pivots based on data (to show expertise).

*The goal is to give their internal marketing lead a "done-for-you" update they can forward directly to their leadership team to champion our work.
